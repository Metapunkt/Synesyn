Using Critical Fabulation as a central conceptual spine, I want us to generate an idea for a 3 minute long video that speaks to the theme of 'fashion intelligence', taking off from a basic idea of a Yoruba Nigerian woman (corporate, middle management type career woman, in her mid thirties) having a casual conversation with her 'Jarvis' (we need to give it a name) like personal assistant in her living room about the outfit she would like to wear to an 'Owambe' party. On the dining table, a contraption (3D printer like 'Outfitter' 'grows' the outfit she is discussing with the AI onto a mannequin shaped after her likeness, in real time, as she speaks, manifesting the exact design she describes to the AI. The Outfiller is out of focus initially while the audience is given time to build context simply from the conversation, then it comes into focus and the lady focuses even more attention onto it by approaching and inspecting it as she continues to speak to the AI, scene ends and morphs by pulling out having the outfit growth sped up to completion and then the mannequin replaced with the real lady as she sashays catwalk style, against an all white background, towards and through the camera. The critical discourse from the Critical Fabulation should sip in through the discourse and other story telling elements, while being well situated within the lady's social, cultural, economic and intellectual realities.
