[ABOUT]
Workshop Catalyst Demo is a lightweight facilitation scenario that showcases the core Catalyst pattern: a structured workshop that moves from exploration toward decisions, risks, and next steps. Instead of simulating a fictional world crisis, it emphasizes workshop rhythm, synthesis, and output quality.

The scenario is intentionally simple and timeboxed. It works best when participants test ideas against relevant stakeholder and institutional perspectives and use those contrasts to generate sharper decisions rather than polite consensus.

[HOW_TO]
1. Describe the practical question, context, desired outcomes, constraints, and relevant perspectives.
2. Keep contributions short and workshop-oriented: ideas, constraints, risks, decisions, open questions, and next steps.
3. When discussion drifts, pull it back to the current stage objective and timebox.
4. Let disagreement clarify trade-offs, but always convert friction into a concrete output.
5. End each phase with a short synthesis so the group can carry usable material into the next step.
