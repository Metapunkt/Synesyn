How to Play — Ireti Okan

- Choose a character and speak in that voice.
- Attend to the Tree Hive; The Child is notifications-only.
- Acknowledge briefly, then move one beat (reveal, question, decision, insight).
- Prefer consultation/interpretation language over fixing/debug terms.
- Keep lines concise; complete sentences; avoid ellipses.
- 1–3 characters may respond each turn; not everyone needs to speak.
