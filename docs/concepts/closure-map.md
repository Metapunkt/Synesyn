# The Closure Map

**From spatial-temporal reduction to recursive closure**

A two-axis instrument for describing how experience, discourse, and systems become
situated, projected, incorporated, and formed. Canonical conceptual companion to
SynAxes (see [ADR 0015](../architectural-decisions/0015-synaxes-v2-closure-map-orientation.md)
and [PRD-SYNAXES-CLOSURE-MAP-ORIENTATION](../product-requirements/PRD-SYNAXES-CLOSURE-MAP-ORIENTATION.md)).

Status: canonical working description (2026-08-07). Not a physical spacetime diagram.

---

## 1. Question

Ordinary spacetime is described as three spatial dimensions plus one temporal
dimension. Reducing that to Temporal ↔ Spatial is asymmetrical: it collapses three
spatial directions into one category and treats time as another kind of extension.

The Closure Map does not reproduce physical spacetime. It asks how a situated
event, object, experience, discourse, or world can be organised through two
tensions that remain meaningful in ordinary spatial and temporal experience.

## 2. Horizontal axis: Past ↔ Future

Past and Future are orientations of closure, not merely two dates.

- **Past** — formed, inherited, retained, remembered, sedimented.
- **Future** — projected, anticipated, possible, forming, not-yet.
- **Now** — the threshold between retention and anticipation. Not a duration among
  other points; the situated moment in which what has been and what may be become
  available to attention.

## 3. Vertical problem

Near ↔ Far fails as a Cartesian axis: ordinary distance satisfies \(d \ge 0\).
There is no negative distance and no condition closer than Here.

Proximal ↔ Distal improves the vocabulary (both are relational to Here-and-Now),
but Proximal still cannot populate an infinite negative side as a quantity. The
negative side must begin when proximity has reached its limit: another form of
extension, not more nearness.

## 4. Resolution: distance becomes depth

The negative value is **distance transformed into depth**.

| Orientation | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Positive (Distal)** | Outward extension — remote, exposed, open-field |
| **Zero (Here-and-Now)** | Proximal minimal closure — situated, immediate |
| **Negative (Deep)** | Deepening / recursive closure — layered, incorporated, latent, resonant |

Distal and Deep do not measure the same thing. Their magnitudes are not directly
comparable; their orientations are structurally related. The Cartesian form is
kept while the asymmetry is admitted honestly.

**Vocabulary**

- **Distal** — outward, remote, extended, exposed, open-field.
- **Here-and-Now** — proximal, situated, immediate, minimal closure.
- **Deep / Recursive Closure** — layered, recursive, incorporated, latent,
  resonant; closure opened inward for further opening, not sealed finish.

## 5. Here-and-Now as double threshold

- Horizontally, Now is the threshold between Past and Future.
- Vertically, Here is maximum proximity and the threshold from which outward
  distance or inward depth begins.

Here-and-Now is where a field becomes situated and where the first closure
occurs: openness provisionally held as this place and this moment. (“Clearing”
was considered; Here-and-Now keeps the spatial-temporal reference while nesting
the deeper meaning.)

## 6. Lawson: first principles

Hilary Lawson treats reality as **openness** exceeding every final capture.
Thought, language, perception, and practice close openness into usable
distinctions.

Here-and-Now is the smallest such closure. From there, closure can extend
outward into a world or horizon, or deepen inward into memory, embodiment,
assumptions, latent consequences, and further closures:

```text
openness → closure → deeper / recursive closure → renewed openness
```

Deep is therefore not Closed in the sense of finished. It is closure made
available for further opening.

## 7. Four quadrants

Lower band label: **Deep / Recursive Closure** (not “Closed”).

|  | **Past** | **Future** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Distal** | **A — Distal Past** · remote inheritance, public history, myth, archive, canon | **B — Distal Future** · projection, speculation, horizon, utopia/dystopia |
| **Deep / Recursive Closure** | **C — Deep Past** · sediment, embodiment, active memory, habit, trauma | **D — Deep Future** · latent potential, intention, formation, tendency |

## 8. Proxistance (the verb)

Distal and Deep are modes; Here-and-Now is the situated origin;
**Proxistance** (Bull.Miletic) is the movement between them — zooming,
navigating, oscillating, recontextualising:

```text
Distal ↔ Proxistance ↔ Deep / Recursive Closure
```

Overview becomes encounter; encounter returns to field. Neither is complete alone.

## 9. Deep Listening

Pauline Oliveros’s Deep Listening clarifies that Deep is not retreat. Depth can
receive the external world more fully by allowing it to become resonant within
the present — “opening within,” not closing off.

- Distal asks: what lies beyond the current closure?
- Deep asks: what becomes available within the current closure when attention
  intensifies?

## 10. Tetrahedron (relations)

Cartesian form shows location. John Wood’s tetrahedron asks which relations are
active among four modes. Vertices A–D; six edges:

| Edge | Reading |
| --- | --- |
| A–B | Inheritance becoming horizon |
| A–C | History becoming embodiment |
| A–D | Inheritance becoming latent potential |
| B–C | Projection returning to memory and constraint |
| B–D | Possibility becoming intention |
| C–D | Sedimented experience becoming becoming |

Mixed cases are trajectories or weighted vertices, not forced into one cell.

## 11. Kind of instrument

Coverage-checking typology, temporal-spatial closure audit, and relational
navigation instrument (akin in function to SWOT or Bartle):

- Coverage, diagnosis, comparison, generation in underrepresented quadrants
- Navigation (Distal ↔ Deep), relational analysis (edges), perceptual calibration
  (Proxistance)

## 12. Structural analogies (non-reductive)

- **Metamodern oscillation** — Distal without Deep becomes naïve projection; Deep
  without Distal becomes repetition or enclosure. Oscillation retains tension.
- **Comparative Yoruba complementarity** — heuristic only: Distal ~ manifest /
  expansive; Deep ~ latent / ancestral potency; Here-and-Now as mediation. Not a
  claim that the map reproduces Yoruba cosmology or Ifá.
- **Transformer attention** — structural correlation only: Here-and-Now ≈ current
  position; Distal ≈ remote context; Deep ≈ recursive layered integration;
  Proxistance ≈ movement between local and global relevance; Closure ≈ provisional
  next-token distribution. Not a claim of machine listening or consciousness.

## 13. Limitations

- Not a physical spacetime diagram.
- Distal and Deep are not equivalent quantities.
- Here-and-Now is minimal closure, not openness itself.
- Deep / Recursive Closure is not sealed interiority.
- Quadrants are not mutually exclusive; edges and trajectories matter.
- Comparative and computational readings are analogies, not identities.

## 14. Synthesis

Every situated act of making sense moves between what is extended beyond the
present and what is recursively held within it. The map does not claim that
experience divides into four compartments; it claims that coverage, diagnosis,
and navigation require naming those orientations and their relations.

## Influences
