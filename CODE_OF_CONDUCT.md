# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Argue with the work, not the person. Assume the other party
knows something you do not.

## Where this applies

Everywhere the project is conducted: this repository's issues, pull requests and
discussions, the [Metapunkt Discord](https://discord.com/invite/fscuH8RmFZ), project
email, and workshops or sessions run under the project's name. It applies to
everyone equally, including the maintainer.

## Expected

- **Engage with the argument.** Disagreement is the point of a design discussion.
  "This breaks the addon boundary and here is why" is welcome; "this is amateur
  work" is not.
- **Accept that the answer is sometimes no.** The project is opinionated and its
  decisions are recorded in [ADRs](docs/architectural-decisions/README.md). A
  rejected proposal is not a slight, and a reasoned "no" is a form of respect.
- **Give people room to be new.** Nobody arrives knowing what a closure is here.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, intimidation, or sustained disruption of a discussion
- Demeaning or exclusionary remarks about people, including about their identity,
  nationality, background, beliefs or circumstances
- Sexual attention or imagery in any project space
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent
- Session content produced deliberately to harass a participant. This project puts
  words in the mouths of AI characters and stages provocative scenarios; that
  latitude exists for the work, not for targeting someone in the room.

## Reporting

Email **metapunkt@metapunkt.org**. Reports are read by Ayodele Arigbabu and are
kept confidential. If your report concerns the maintainer and you would rather not
write to him, say so in the message and an independent third party will be arranged
before the matter is taken further.

Include what happened, where, and roughly when. You do not need to have documented
it perfectly, and you do not need to be certain it "counts".

## Consequences

Depending on severity: a private word, a public correction, removal of the content,
a temporary exclusion, or a permanent ban from project spaces. Decisions are
explained to the person affected.

## Basis

This adapts the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
rather than reproducing its text, so that it can say something specific about the
things this project actually does. Where a case is unclear, the Covenant is the
reference.
